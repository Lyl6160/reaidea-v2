## Evidence rules

- Founder visual approval must identify the exact file, version, path, hash and approval scope.
- Temporary browser captures may be indexed only when retained in an approved external archive; they never become authority automatically.
- Rejected or superseded evidence remains labelled and cannot silently re-enter production.
- A hash proves file identity, not product truth, safety, engineering quality or founder acceptance.
- Link existing architecture, construction and daily records instead of repeating their contents.
