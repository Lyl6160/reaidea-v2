# reAIdea Architecture Decisions

**Status:** FOUNDER APPROVED — supporting decision record
**Date:** Accepted 26 August 2026
**Authority:** `architecture/SUPERSESSION_REGISTER.md` determines current supersession. These decisions retain rationale and do not delete historical journals.

## AD-001 — Separate founder authority from construction plans

The Founder Product Constitution owns durable product truth. The Flight Plan owns construction sequence. Construction state and handovers remain implementation evidence only.

## AD-002 — Long questionnaires are not the default journey

Origin intent, one natural-language description and optional evidence replace mandatory Discovery/Interview sequences as the normal entry. Legacy routes remain until the founder decides retirement or repurposing.

## AD-003 — Tell REV Once and one smallest question

Accepted information follows one Project. REV routes source-traced relevance to several benches and asks at most one smallest question that materially changes or safely unblocks useful work.

## AD-004 — Creation completes before Workshop navigation

The initial transaction is safety preflight → save/verify Project → generate → validate/bind → persist/reload verify → navigate. Failure stays on Home and retry is deliberate.

## AD-005 — Evidence and assumptions remain separate

Inventor evidence, REV understanding, REV assumptions, generated concepts, external evidence and accepted Project truth use distinct contracts. Only deliberate canonical writers change accepted truth.

## AD-006 — Visual Concept is the truthful 3D fallback

Only validated source-bound ConceptGeometry may appear as Interactive 3D. A valid Visual Concept occupies centre stage when geometry is unavailable, invalid or mismatched.

## AD-007 — One Canvas and centre-podium ownership

Workshop owns one Prototype3DViewer mount and one mutually exclusive Canvas inline or in the accessible portal-backed dialog. Controls may portal; the model and Canvas remain podium-owned.

## AD-008 — Outcome-delivery product model

The intended journey is choose intent → describe once → truthful creation tracking → delivered Concept/Workshop evidence → one next useful decision. Product language is low-friction, outcome-first and respectful.

## AD-009 — No fake progress or certainty

Progress follows real operations. Percentages, countdowns, confidence theatre, patentability, engineering approval and market proof require real supporting evidence or are not shown.

## AD-010 — Provider-independent, safety-first orchestration

reAIdea owns workflow, policy, schemas and continuity. Providers remain replaceable. Text and image paths must CLEAR before persistence/generation, and generation repeats safety checks.

## AD-011 — Project is authoritative; Digital Twin is subordinate

One Project owns canonical truth and history. Any Digital Twin is a Project-owned derived read model, not a second store, timeline or cross-Project memory.

## AD-012 — Historical records remain immutable evidence

Founder journals, old blueprints, handovers and visual checkpoints remain unchanged. Current authority may supersede their instructions without erasing the record.

## AD-013 — Project 000 governs self-development

reAIdea applies the same eight-bench evidence, assumption, limitation and decision discipline to itself. Roadmap movement and founder enthusiasm are not product, security or market proof.

## AD-014 — One active build lane

Each Build Contract names one Lane 0–7. Cross-lane work requires an accepted decision explaining why separation is unsafe and defining a combined boundary, acceptance and rollback.

## AD-015 — AGENTS is the gateway, not product authority

The canonical session order is AGENTS → Context → Active Handoff → Constitution → Flight Plan → relevant page/visual authority → Current Construction State → active Build Contract. AGENTS enforces this order and cannot change product direction.

## AD-016 — Home and Workshop progress use different truth contracts

Home understanding/readiness uses no percentages and makes no confidence, feasibility or probability-of-success claim. Workshop progress may use percentages only when calculated from real canonical Project state; static, invented or unsupported values remain prohibited.

## AD-017 — Three founder-approved visual foundations

`VPB-HOME-001`, sign-free `VPB-WORKSHOP-001` and `VPB-JOURNEY-001` control their scoped visual direction. The journey is Home → verified creation/persistence → Workshop navigation → current invention on the existing one-Canvas podium. STOP/GO remains historical sample content only.

## AD-018 — Home AI understanding precedes stored-3D creation

`VPB-HOME-002`, `VPB-HOME-003` and `VPB-JOURNEY-002` establish the current initial journey: describe once → `ASK REV` → one useful question at a time → evidence-derived knowledge growth → `READY TO CREATE 3D` → truthful Creation Theatre → validated and reload-verified Concept 01 geometry → deliberate `ENTER WORKSHOP` → the same stored 3D on the centre podium. The understanding meter measures captured information rather than confidence or feasibility. This decision supersedes automatic creation after one description, automatic Workshop navigation and Visual-Concept-only completion for the initial canonical journey, while preserving earlier authorities as non-conflicting visual and historical evidence.
