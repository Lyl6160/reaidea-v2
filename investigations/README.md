# Purpose

This folder contains engineering investigations into new ideas, concepts and architectural directions.

Investigations exist to challenge ideas before they become engineering standards.

Every investigation asks one fundamental question:

**Does this idea genuinely improve engineering understanding?**

If the answer is uncertain, the investigation continues.

If the answer is yes, the concept may progress toward blueprint development.

---

# Why Investigations Exist

Engineering innovation should never rely upon assumptions alone.

Every major concept should be questioned.

Every architectural proposal should be challenged.

Every significant idea should earn its place through disciplined engineering review.

Investigations reduce risk before implementation begins.

# Engineering Principles

Investigations exist to discover truth.

Not to confirm existing beliefs.

Evidence is valued above opinion.

Questions are valued above assumptions.

Engineering discipline requires curiosity before certainty.

Every investigation should leave the project with greater understanding than when it began.

---

# Repository Relationship

Foundations explain why we exist.

Investigations explore what may become possible.

Blueprints define what has been approved.

Standards define how we work.

Implementation builds the product.

---

# Closing Statement

Innovation without investigation creates risk.

Investigation without engineering creates theory.

reAIdea combines disciplined investigation with disciplined engineering.
