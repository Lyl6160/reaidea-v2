# Engineering Standards

# Purpose

This folder contains the engineering standards that govern how reAIdea is designed, developed and maintained.

Standards exist to create consistency.

Consistency creates quality.

Quality builds trust.

Engineering standards are permanent references that apply across the entire organisation.

---

# Why Standards Exist

As projects grow, different engineers naturally develop different habits.

Without standards:

• Documentation becomes inconsistent.

• Software quality varies.

• Engineering decisions become difficult to review.

• Knowledge becomes fragmented.

Standards provide a common engineering language.

Blueprints

---

# Engineering Philosophy

Standards do not exist to restrict engineering.

They exist to improve consistency.

Consistency allows engineers to focus on solving problems instead of interpreting different ways of working.

---

# Repository Relationship

Foundations explain why.

Investigations explore possibilities.

Blueprints define architecture.

Standards define how engineering is performed.

Implementation builds the product.

---

# Engineering Principle

Every standard should make engineering easier.

If a standard creates unnecessary complexity, it should be reviewed.

Good standards reduce cognitive effort.

They do not increase it.

---

# Closing Statement

Consistency is one of the foundations of engineering excellence.

The standards within this folder exist to preserve the quality of reAIdea as it continues to evolve.
