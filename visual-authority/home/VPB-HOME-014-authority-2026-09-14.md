## Runtime and accessibility boundary

The three PNGs are decorative assets only. Pathway cards, titles, descriptions, borders, selection controls, states and accessible naming remain live semantic HTML/CSS. Runtime images must use empty alternative text and `aria-hidden="true"`, while the interactive control owns its accessible name and description. Responsive rendering uses one identical source per pathway with proportional `object-fit: contain`; it must not crop, stretch, recolour, sharpen or regenerate the assets.

The in-room candidate and comparisons are visual evidence only. They are not runtime screenshots, complete interface assets or authority to flatten the console.

Rejected SVG candidates remain rejected temporary evidence and are not authority.
