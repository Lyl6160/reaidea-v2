## Founder intent captured

The invention is sunglasses-first surf eyewear: visually familiar premium sports sunglasses enhanced with a soft eye-area seal and a secure rear retaining strap for surf and brief underwater use.

## Source-backed must-haves

- Low-profile sunglasses presentation on the face.
- Broad mirrored aqua/blue eyewear.
- Normal open nose bridge and fully exposed lower face.
- Soft gasket concealed behind the frame and limited to the eye area.
- Sunglass-style side arms transitioning into an adjustable rear strap.
- Clear relationship to real surf use.

## Must-avoid form

- Conventional scuba or diving mask.
- Nose pocket, nose cup or nose-enclosing body.
- Full-face mask, snorkel, ski-goggle or helmet form.
- Bulky single sealed-mask silhouette.

## Truth boundary

- This image is founder-approved visual interpretation, not proof of sealing, optics, fit, durability, safety, manufacturability or engineering dimensions.
- Materials, dimensions, proportions and performance remain assumptions until separately evidenced.
- The source photograph was supporting form/use evidence and did not transfer ownership or engineering truth to REV.

## General-system rule

This fidelity target is an acceptance fixture for source-aware Concept creation. It must not create a surf-eyewear special case. The system must generalise the same behaviour to other Projects by preserving inventor-authored comparisons, typed must-haves and must-avoids, provenance-labelled visual-form evidence and truthful candidate validation.

## Acceptance use

A future C2F candidate passes this fixture only when it:

1. carries the sunglasses-first silhouette and open-nose topology into the Concept request;
2. uses cleared visual evidence as supportive form evidence without overriding inventor intent;
3. rejects or questions a conventional scuba-mask interpretation before persistence;
4. parameterises any supported 3D geometry from accepted evidence and labelled reversible assumptions;
5. falls back truthfully when fidelity cannot be established;
6. never claims image-to-3D conversion or engineering readiness.
