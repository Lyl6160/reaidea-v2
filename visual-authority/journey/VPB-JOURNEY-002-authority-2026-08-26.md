- Preserve the accepted Home scene, REV identity, headline hierarchy and integrated console.
- Present `WHAT REV UNDERSTANDS` from captured, source-distinguished Project information.
- Ask one useful question at a time and never repeat accepted information.
- Offer `I'M NOT SURE — LET REV RECOMMEND` only when REV can make an evidence-backed recommendation without guessing.
- Use the corrected promise: “REV will understand it, create your first 3D concept and prepare your Workshop.”
- Images remain optional supporting evidence; the inventor's description remains authoritative.

## VPB-HOME-003 — knowledge-pulse architecture

- Blue electrical energy represents active communication from REV's hand.
- Orange energy represents newly accepted and secured knowledge entering the next meter segment.
- Meter advancement occurs only after information coverage genuinely increases and is recorded to the same Project.
- The meter measures captured information, not confidence, feasibility, probability of success or engineering completeness.
- No timer, percentage, countdown, random pulse or decorative animation may claim knowledge growth.
- Reduced-motion users receive a calm, non-moving equivalent with the same textual state.

## VPB-JOURNEY-002 — Creation Theatre and Workshop handover

- The Creation Theatre remains on Home while REV creates, checks fidelity, validates geometry, persists the exact Concept/candidate/geometry and verifies reload.
- Activity messages correspond only to real operations; animation may continue during a pending operation but cannot imply completion.
- `ENTER WORKSHOP` becomes available only after validated, source-bound Concept 01 geometry is durably stored and reload-verified.
- Workshop arrival restores that same persisted model without regeneration.
- The Workshop-arrival composition requires REV with both hands extended, the current invention centred on the podium and no empty-podium arrival.

- A clean, separately rendered two-hands REV layer remains a future implementation dependency and is not required to register this composition authority.
