## Controls and interaction

The three intent choices are native radio controls presented as filled cards. None is preselected. Before selection, the REV entry panel is visually subdued and its textarea, image control and `ASK REV` action are disabled. Selection gives the chosen card a visible selected mark, activates the REV panel and moves keyboard focus to the description. The selection does not create a Project, persist data, call an API or invoke a provider.

`ASK REV` remains disabled until intent and description exist and any selected image is valid. `HOW IT WORKS` and `WHAT REV DOES` remain in-page links. `RETURNING PROJECT` is absent from `BLANK NEW PROJECT`; a future Project Library requires its own authority and authorization design.
