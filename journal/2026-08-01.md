# Founder Journal

Date: 1 August 2026

---

## Today Changed Everything

Today we stopped thinking about reAIdea as software.

We started thinking about it as an experience.

This may become the most important decision in the entire project.

---

## The Biggest Realisation

People do not remember software.

People remember experiences.

The objective is no longer to build an AI that asks questions.

The objective is to create an engineering partner that helps inventors build successful products.

---

## Discovery

The word "Interview" no longer reflects the experience.

The process is now called:

Project Discovery

This is no longer a questionnaire.

It is a guided engineering journey.

---

## Questions

Questions are no longer numbered.

There will never again be:

Question 1

Question 2

Question 3

Instead every interaction becomes a Mission.

Example

Mission

Discover the Problem

Mission

Identify the Customer

Mission

Evaluate Existing Solutions

Mission

Define Your Competitive Advantage

---

## Every Mission

Every Mission must contain:

Mission Brief

Why This Matters

Your Assignment

AI Reflection

Mission Complete

Next Mission

No exceptions.

The inventor should always understand WHY they are doing something.

---

## Experience

The AI must never feel robotic.

The AI should acknowledge what it has learned before asking for more information.

Every response should feel like a conversation with an experienced engineer.

---

## Project Core

The Project remains the centre of everything.

Not the interview.

Not the dashboard.

Not Patent AI.

Everything learns from one Project.

---

## Product Philosophy

We are not building software.

We are building confidence.

Confidence comes from understanding.

Understanding comes from Discovery.

---

## One Important Quote

"It has to be an experience rather than a robotic progressive step to understand the invention."

This sentence became one of the guiding principles of reAIdea.

---

## Next Objective

Replace question-driven interaction with mission-driven discovery.

Allow the AI to explain:

What it learned.

Why it matters.

What remains unknown.

Why the next mission exists.

---

## Reflection

Today felt less like software engineering...

...and more like designing the future culture of a company.
