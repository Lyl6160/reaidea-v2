reAIdea Company Charter

Document ID: CHARTER-001Version: 1.0 DraftStatus: Under Whiteboard Review

Mission

To improve engineering thinking by systematically reducing uncertainty.

Vision

To create the world's most trusted engineering thinking platform for inventors, engineers, investors, manufacturers, researchers and decision-makers.

Purpose

reAIdea does not create ideas for people.

reAIdea helps people understand, challenge, strengthen and develop their ideas until responsible engineering decisions can be made.

Core Philosophy

Observe.Begin with what has been seen, experienced or questioned.

Walk Around It.Examine the same idea from multiple relevant perspectives before committing to a direction.

KIS — Keep It Simple.Complexity must earn its place.

Build With Evidence.Engineering confidence must come from reason, evidence and validation.

Operating Principle

reAIdea exists to strengthen the Engineering State.

Every bench, perspective, interaction and system behaviour must leave the Engineering State clearer, stronger or better supported than it was before.

Workshop Model

reAIdea is experienced as one engineering workshop.

The user remains at the centre of the workshop.

Specialist benches, perspectives and tools move around the user as required.

The workshop is coordinated by a Foreman.

The Foreman routes work, manages readiness and introduces the right specialist capability at the right time.

One Thinking Engine

reAIdea uses one Thinking Engine.

The engine does not change between inventor, investor, manufacturer or other perspectives.

The mission, questions, tone and specialist focus may change.

The underlying Engineering State remains the single source of truth.

Ownership

The Engineering State belongs to the user.

reAIdea acts as its custodian and engineering partner.

The platform must preserve user ownership, project continuity and engineering integrity.

Cultural Standard

reAIdea challenges ideas to strengthen inventors, not to defeat them.

The workshop must be rigorous without becoming destructive.

Understanding comes before challenge.

Evidence comes before confidence.

Readiness comes before commitment.

Decision Filter

Before adding any feature, document, module or behaviour, ask:

Why does this exist?

What uncertainty does it reduce?

How does it improve engineering thinking?

Can KIS remove or simplify it?

Which part of the workshop owns the responsibility?

If those questions cannot be answered clearly, it should not be built.

Workshop Motto

Observe.Walk Around It.Keep It Simple.Build With Evidence.
