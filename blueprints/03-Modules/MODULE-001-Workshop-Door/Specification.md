Purpose

Create the first experience of reAIdea.

The Workshop Door exists to make a person feel they have entered a professional engineering workshop where ideas are respected and understanding comes before judgement.

Responsibility

The Workshop Door has one responsibility only:

Begin the engineering journey.

It does not analyse, engineer, validate or challenge ideas.

Engineering Questions

Why does this module exist?

To reduce uncertainty for first-time users and encourage them to share their first observation.

What uncertainty does it reduce?

'Am I in the right place?'

What happens if this module is removed?

The workshop loses its identity and becomes another generic AI interface.
