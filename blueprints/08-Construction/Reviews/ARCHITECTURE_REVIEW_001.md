# Major Architectural Discoveries

During engineering review several concepts emerged that were not part of the original project vision.

These discoveries significantly changed the architecture of reAIdea.

Discovery One

Knowledge and Understanding are different.

Knowledge stores facts.

Understanding interprets facts.

Reasoning depends upon understanding rather than raw information.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Discovery Two

Conversation is an engineering capability.

Questions collect information.

Conversations create understanding.

Every engine should inherit a consistent communication philosophy.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Discovery Three

Discovery produces understanding.

Understanding should become a structured engineering asset.

This resulted in the Engineering Discovery Profile.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Discovery Four

Projects should preserve engineering understanding rather than conversation history.

This insight introduced the concept of the Engineering Digital Twin.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

# Engineering Assessment

The Engineering Digital Twin is no longer considered a supporting feature.

It is now recognised as a central architectural component of the reAIdea platform.

Every specialist engine should contribute to its continuous improvement.

Every specialist engine should reason from its current state.

The Digital Twin becomes the living engineering representation of every project.
