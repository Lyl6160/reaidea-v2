# reAIdea Approved Home + Workshop Visual Direction

Date: 18 August 2026
Status: APPROVED VISUAL NORTH STAR — BUILD 3 COMPLETE; VISUAL IMPLEMENTATION REMAINS A SEPARATE APPROVED BUILD

## Founder decision

The Home screen and Living Workshop visual direction are locked.

The intended experience is not a conventional software dashboard. The inventor enters a living engineering environment where their idea becomes visible, REV remains present, and every bench feels ready to help develop the invention.

## Approved Home direction

Approved asset:

`reAIdea_Home_REV_Engineer_Approved_2026-08-18.png`

REV replaces the generic human host. REV is presented as a non-human engineering intelligence with:

- the faceless black-and-blue geometric wireframe head;
- `R.E.V.` identity lettering;
- the geometric shoulder badge;
- a practical rolled-sleeve engineer's shirt;
- dark jeans and polished leather shoes;
- the leather engineering notebook with its embossed insignia;
- an open hand welcoming the inventor forward;
- blue footsteps leading into the Workshop.

The Home form occupies the quieter central area. The surrounding environment is bright enough to feel welcoming while retaining the premium blue-and-charcoal reAIdea identity.

The leather notebook and shoulder badge are permanent REV identity elements.

## Locked Home amendment — 19 August 2026

Additional reference asset:

`reAIdea_Home_Wall_Branding_Reference_2026-08-19.png`

The future Home wall must visibly carry:

- the exact `reAIdea` wordmark, with the `AI` emphasis preserved;
- `REALISE • ENGINEER • VALIDATE` as the permanent meaning of `REV`;
- `REV` presented as the AI Engineering Partner identity.

The additional reference is approved for wall-branding placement and wording only. Its human guide is not approved for the future Home. The approved wireframe REV engineer, clothing, badge, leather notebook and welcoming gesture remain authoritative.

The Home doorway must no longer reveal the older dark workshop shown in the branding reference. It must visually open into, or closely mimic, the bright new Living Workshop north star so Home and Workshop feel like one continuous place. Lighting, architecture, floor path and doorway perspective should make the transition believable.

The central wall must still leave enough quiet visual space for the live Home form. Brand lettering must support the form rather than compete with it.

## Approved Workshop direction

Approved asset:

`reAIdea_Workshop_Visual_North_Star_Approved_2026-08-18.png`

The Living Workshop uses:

- a bright, believable physical engineering workshop;
- distinct functional benches surrounding the same active Project;
- the Workshop remaining visible while a bench is active;
- a practical foreground console for active work;
- the inventor's reference, Concept 01, what REV already knows, what REV prepared, and one smallest question;
- REV as the continuing AI Engineering Partner;
- the active invention visibly occupying the centre of the Workshop;
- an output-first path from invention to engineering reality.

The example STOP/GO content and technical claims in the visual are illustrative only. The implemented interface must display current Project truth and source-traced REV understanding. Nothing shown in the concept artwork automatically becomes Project truth.

## Locked design principles

- KIS
- Tell REV Once
- REV Does More Work
- Output-First Bench
- Smallest Question
- No Repetition
- Workshop always visible
- Current Project truth boundaries
- VisualDesignSnapshot remains non-authoritative
- ConceptGeometry and safe 3D foundation preserved
- Provider independence
- The inventor owns the invention; REV has the helm

## Deferred visual work

These details may be refined when visual implementation begins:

- wall visualisations and their exact content;
- final bench proportions and spacing;
- typography and responsive scaling;
- console polish and interaction animation;
- lighting balance at different screen sizes;
- production cropping, compression and asset optimisation.

The wall branding and bright-Workshop doorway continuity are not deferred optional details; they are required implementation constraints. Only their final scale, spacing and responsive treatment may be refined.

Deferred refinements must preserve the approved Home REV identity, the bright physical Workshop, practical console language, and always-visible Workshop structure.
